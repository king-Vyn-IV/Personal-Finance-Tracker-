Personal Finance Tracker 💰

Personal Finance Tracker is a mobile application designed to simplify personal money management. It allows users to record income and expenses, organize transactions, monitor their balance, and gain better insights into their spending habits.

The goal is to provide a simple and intuitive financial management experience that helps users make smarter financial decisions and stay on top of their money.

Features
💰 Dashboard – View your financial overview at a glance.

📊 Expense Tracking – Record and manage daily expenses.

💵 Income Tracking – Keep track of your income sources.

📅 Transaction History – View and manage past transactions.

🏷️ Categories – Organize transactions into different categories.

📈 Spending Insights – Monitor spending patterns and financial habits.

🧮 Balance Overview – Track your income, expenses, and remaining balance.
